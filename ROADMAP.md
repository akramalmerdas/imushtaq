# [Product Roadmap: SEO Audit and Enhancement]

## 1. Vision and Tech Stack
* **Problem:** The website's organic traffic and search engine rankings need to be improved by identifying and addressing on-page, technical, and off-page SEO issues.
* **Proposed Solution:** A comprehensive SEO audit will be conducted, and a detailed report with actionable recommendations will be provided.
* **Tech Stack:** I will use a combination of my built-in tools (`google_search`, `view_text_website`, `read_file`, `ls`) to perform the audit. The final report will be a text-based document.
* **Applied Constraints and Preferences:** The audit will be conducted based on the "Code Gear-1" protocol outlined in `AGENTS.md`, with the audit process broken down into functional modules.

## 2. Core Requirements (from Fact Research)
*   **On-Page SEO:** Optimize content, titles, meta descriptions, and headers for relevant keywords.
*   **Technical SEO:** Ensure the site is crawlable, indexable, fast, mobile-friendly, and uses structured data correctly.
*   **Off-Page SEO:** Develop a strategy for acquiring high-quality backlinks.

## 3. Prioritized Functional Modules (designed to meet the above requirements)
| Priority | Functional Module | Rationale (from research) | Description (includes grouped features) |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| 1 | **On-Page SEO Analysis** | On-page factors are a great starting point as they are directly controllable and form the foundation of any solid SEO strategy. | I will analyze the website's title tags, meta descriptions, header tags (H1, H2, etc.), and body content. I will also perform keyword research to identify high-impact opportunities. |
| 2 | **Technical SEO Audit** | Technical issues can severely hinder a website's visibility in search results and negatively impact user experience. | I will check the `robots.txt` file, look for a sitemap, assess the site's mobile-friendliness, and check for the correct implementation of structured data (schema markup). |
| 3. **Backlink Analysis** | Backlinks from reputable sources are a powerful signal of a website's authority and are crucial for achieving competitive rankings. | I will identify key competitors, analyze their backlink profiles to uncover opportunities, and propose a tailored strategy for acquiring high-quality backlinks. |
| 4. **Final Report Generation** | A clear, consolidated report is essential for understanding the audit's findings and implementing the recommended changes effectively. | I will create a comprehensive report that summarizes all my findings and provides a prioritized list of actionable recommendations for on-page, technical, and off-page SEO improvements. |
